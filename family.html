Kids & Families

Family Ministry

Discipling children in the ways of Jesus — at home, in community, and together on Sundays.

Our Philosophy

Parents as primary disciplers

We believe God has called parents to raise their children in the ways and truths of God. Parents are primarily responsible for training their children to live out their gospel identity in everyday rhythms of life as followers of Jesus — and in this way both parents and children grow in the knowledge and grace of our Lord Jesus.

This means we see discipleship for our kids happening in the everyday activities of life, as our families live gospel-centered lives on Jesus' mission. We want our kids to grow up seeing people of all walks of life learning to live in the ways of grace — discipled in our homes, in our neighborhoods, and in our regular lives.

When we gather as a whole church on Sundays, there are special opportunities for our children to be together as they grow and learn how to walk in the ways of Jesus. Each class is a combination of age-appropriate Bible stories, games, and activities, all focused on helping our kids understand God's Story, believe God's Son, and walk as God's people.

Serve

How can you help?

Teacher

Lead a class through age-appropriate Bible stories, activities, and gospel-centered discussion.

Helper

Support the lead teacher and assist with crafts, games, and caring for kids during class.

Kids Check-In

Welcome families at the door, help with secure check-in, and make the drop-off experience smooth.

Nursery

Care for our youngest — infants through 24 months — while their parents worship in the main service.

A background check is required for all family ministry volunteers. Email us to get started.
