Generosity

Give

Giving is an act of worship — a tangible way to partner in the work of the Gospel in Fairbanks and beyond.

Why We Give

Generosity as discipleship

We believe that how we handle money is a spiritual matter. Giving is one of the most concrete ways we loosen our grip on the things of this world and express trust in God's provision and kingdom purposes.

Your giving supports the ministry of Radiant Church — Sunday gatherings, Gospel Communities, Family Ministry, pastoral care, and our commitment to seeing Fairbanks reached with the Gospel. It also supports the broader Plant AK network of church planting throughout Alaska.

All gifts are tax-deductible. You will receive a giving statement for tax purposes.
