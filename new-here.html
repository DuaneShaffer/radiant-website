New Here? Welcome.

We're glad you're considering visiting. Here's everything you need to feel at home before you walk through the door.

The Basics

What to know before you come

WhenSundays at 11:00 AM. Services typically last about 75–80 minutes.
Where949 Farmers Loop Road, Fairbanks, AK 99709. Free parking on site.
What to WearCome as you are — you'll see everything from jeans to Sunday best. There's no dress code.
For Your KidsFamily Ministry is available for children during the service. Check-in is easy through the Church Center app.
The ServiceExpect contemporary worship music, communion every Sunday, and a Bible-based message that's both honest and accessible.

The Experience

What a Sunday looks like

Arrive & Connect

Plan to arrive a few minutes early. Our welcome team will greet you at the door, help with kids' check-in, and point you to coffee. No pressure to introduce yourself — just settle in.

Worship Together

We sing, take communion, and give — all as acts of worship. If singing isn't your thing, just stand with us. The words are displayed on screen and there's no performance expected.

Hear the Word

The message is the heart of the service — a verse-by-verse walk through Scripture that's honest, practical, and centered on the Gospel. Bring a Bible, or follow along on your phone.

Common Questions

You probably have questions
