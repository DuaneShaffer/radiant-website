You don't have to face hard things alone. We are a church family that prays, mourns, and rejoices together.

Pastoral Care

We care for one another

Life brings seasons of joy and seasons of deep difficulty — illness, loss, broken relationships, doubt, and grief. Radiant's elders and care team are committed to walking alongside members and regular attenders through whatever they're facing.

If you are going through something hard and need someone to talk to, pray with, or visit you — please reach out. No need is too small and no situation too complex. We want to point you to Christ and stand with you.
