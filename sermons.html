Psalms

The Psalms are the prayerbook of God's people — songs of praise, lament, confession, and trust that have shaped the worship of the church for thousands of years. We're working through a selection of Psalms, letting their words become our words as we learn to bring all of life before God.

Taught by Ben Widman.
