About Radiant

Our Mission

The glory of God, seen in the Gospel

We believe the glory of God is seen most beautifully in the Gospel of Jesus Christ. Our invitation is to be part of this church family — fully engaged, walking in relationship with God and with each other as we live as a family of servant missionaries making disciples that make disciples.

The church has a clear biblical mandate to look beyond its own community to the neighborhood, the nation, and the world. Mission is not an optional program; it is an essential element of who we are. We exist to equip the saints for ministry and to seek Jesus being known in our local community.

We are a member church of A29 AK, a network of gospel-centered churches committed to planting and strengthening churches throughout Alaska.

"Our vision is to be a radiant reflection of the gospel to the Fairbanks North Star Borough by living out our identity in Jesus through our Sunday Gatherings, our Gospel Communities, and with other Gospel-centered churches and ministries. We intentionally partner with other Radiant Churches in Alaska to see Gospel saturation spread across the North and beyond."
— Radiant Church Family Vision

The Gospel

The Gospel is the good news of what God has graciously accomplished for sinners through the sinless life, sacrificial death, and bodily resurrection of His Son, our Savior, Jesus Christ — our forgiveness from sin and complete justification before God. Christ's penal substitutionary death and bodily resurrection are central to our message.

God's Sovereignty

We believe God acts sovereignly in saving sinners. Salvation is not achieved by human effort but received by grace — God's grace both saves and sanctifies. We rest in the truth that He is the author and perfecter of our faith.

The Holy Spirit

We believe the continuing presence of the Holy Spirit equips and empowers the saints. It is through the Spirit that we are transformed into the image of Christ, empowered for mission, and drawn into deeper relationship with God.

Image Bearers

We believe God created men and women as equal in value and complementary image bearers of God. This shapes how we understand human dignity, the family, and the distinct and honoring roles men and women play in the home and the church.

Mission

We believe all believers are sent on Jesus' mission to make disciples. The church is not a place to consume but a people on mission — called to bring the Gospel to bear on every dimension of life, in Fairbanks and beyond.

Leadership

Elders & Staff

Radiant is an elder-led, elder-governed church. Elders are examples in their character — ministers of the Word committed to prayer and to watching over the spiritual condition of the church. Among the elder team, one elder functions as the lead of the team.
