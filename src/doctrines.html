Five Core Emphases

What shapes us as a church

Radiant Church is committed to the Lausanne Covenant Statement of Faith. From that foundation, five theological convictions shape everything we do:

  1. The Gospel of Jesus Christ is central to our motivation and practice.
  2. God acts sovereignly in saving sinners.
  3. The continuing presence of the Holy Spirit equips and empowers the saints.
  4. God created men and women as equal in value and complementary image bearers of God.
  5. All believers are sent on Jesus' mission to make disciples.

Full Statement

Our doctrinal convictions

Each conviction below reflects our understanding of Scripture and shapes how we teach, lead, and live together.

The Gospel

The Gospel is the good news of what God has graciously accomplished for sinners through the sinless life, sacrificial death, and bodily resurrection of His Son, our Savior, Jesus Christ. Through Christ we receive forgiveness from sin and complete justification before God. Christ's penal substitutionary death and bodily resurrection are central to the Gospel we proclaim.

The Gospel is not only the means by which people are saved, but the truth and power by which they are sanctified. It is central to our motivation, our community, and our mission.

Key texts: Rom. 1:16–17; 1 Cor. 15:3–4; 2 Cor. 5:21

Salvation by Grace Alone

We enthusiastically embrace the sovereignty of God's grace in saving sinners. Salvation is received by grace alone, through faith alone, in Christ alone. No ordinance, ritual, work, or any other activity on the part of man is required in order to be saved.

God chose us in Christ before the foundation of the world — not on the basis of foreseen faith, but unconditionally. His Spirit effectively draws the elect to faith, and those He has chosen will persevere in belief and godly behavior and be kept secure. This sovereignty does not diminish human responsibility to believe, nor does it lessen our urgency in prayer and evangelism.

Key texts: Eph. 2:8–9; Eph. 1:4–5; John 10:28–29

The Holy Spirit

We recognize and rest upon the necessity of the empowering presence of the Holy Spirit for all life and ministry. The Spirit is fully God, equal with the Father and the Son, whose primary ministry is to glorify the Lord Jesus Christ.

The Holy Spirit indwells every believer, sanctifies them progressively, and distributes spiritual gifts to each member of the body for ministry and the growth of the church. We affirm that these gifts are divine provisions central to spiritual growth and effective ministry, while recognizing that Christians hold diverse and sincere views about their nature and practice.

Key texts: John 14:16–17; 1 Cor. 12:7–11; Gal. 5:22–23

Men, Women, and the Church

We believe God created men and women as equal in value and complementary image bearers. Both men and women possess equal dignity before God, equal access to Him through Christ, and both receive spiritual gifts for ministry in the church and the world.

At the same time, we believe the office of elder is reserved for men who meet the qualifications set out in Scripture. Male headship in the home is to be characterized by loving, tender, and nurturing care — servant-leadership and sacrificial love modeled after Christ's love for the church.

Key texts: Gen. 1:27; 1 Tim. 2:12; 1 Tim. 3:1–7; Eph. 5:25–33

Mission

Radiant Church embraces a missionary understanding of the local church. We are called to make Christ known through the Gospel and, by the power of the Holy Spirit, to bring His lordship to bear on every dimension of life.

Mission is not an optional program — it is an essential element in the identity of the church. We exist to equip the saints for ministry and to seek Jesus being known in our local community, across Alaska, and to the ends of the earth. We are a member of A29 AK, a network of gospel-centered churches committed to planting and strengthening churches throughout Alaska.

Key texts: Matt. 28:18–20; Acts 1:8; Eph. 4:11–12
