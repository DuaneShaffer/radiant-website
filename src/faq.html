Frequently Asked Questions

Answers to the most common things people wonder before visiting or getting connected at Radiant.

Visiting for the First Time

We gather every Sunday at 11:00 AM at 949 Farmers Loop Road, Fairbanks, AK 99709. Free parking is available on site. Services typically run about 75–80 minutes. We're a congregation of around 100 adults — mostly young families — with a casual-to-Alaskan-formal dress culture.

Here's the flow of a typical Sunday:

  1. Two opening worship songs
  2. Prayer
  3. Another song
  4. Children's dismissal to Family Ministry classes
  5. Bible reading
  6. Sermon
  7. Communion (taken together during the first song after the sermon)
  8. Two closing songs
  9. Final prayer

Not at all. Wherever you are on your spiritual journey — curious, skeptical, exploring, or somewhere in between — you are genuinely welcome here. We don't expect you to have everything figured out before you show up.

Come as you are — seriously. You'll see everything from jeans and hoodies to button-downs and dresses. There is no dress code, and no one is tracking what you wore.

We have Family Ministry available for every age during the Sunday service:

  • Radiant Babies — ages 0–24 months
  • Radiant Kids — ages 2–9
  • Radiant Youth — ages 10 and up (meets 2nd & 4th Sundays)

Kids check in via the Church Center app — first-timers can check in with our welcome team on arrival. You're also welcome to keep your children with you in the main service.

Giving is part of our Sunday worship, but it's for regular members and attenders — not guests. We have giving boxes on the communion tables — guests are welcome to walk right past them. No pressure, no guilt.

What We Believe

Radiant is a non-denominational, gospel-centered church — a family of servant missionaries committed to making disciples. We are a member of A29 AK (Acts 29 Alaska), a network of churches committed to planting and strengthening gospel-centered congregations throughout Alaska. Our theology is broadly Reformed and evangelical, with strong convictions about grace, Scripture, and the centrality of the Gospel.

We believe that salvation is received by grace alone, through faith alone, in Christ alone. No ordinance, ritual, work, or activity on the part of man is required in order to be saved. Christ's penal substitutionary death and bodily resurrection are central to the Gospel we proclaim.
