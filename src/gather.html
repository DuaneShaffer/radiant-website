What to Expect

Why we gather

Every Sunday we come together with three purposes — to proclaim, to participate, and to be sent out.

Proclaim

Every Sunday we hear a message from the Bible that points to who God is, what He has done, and how that changes our identities and how we live our lives.

Participate

We center our lives and affections on the person of Jesus — through singing, communion, conversations with one another, and giving to God's mission together.

Mobilize

We equip our people to make disciples during the week — encouraging everyone on a path of discipleship, sending them out to live the Gospel in their neighborhoods and daily lives.
