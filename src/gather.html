What to Expect

What a Sunday looks like

Arrive & Connect

Plan to arrive a few minutes early. Our welcome team will greet you at the door, help with kids' check-in, and point you to coffee. No pressure to introduce yourself — just settle in.

Worship Together

We sing, take communion, and give — all as acts of worship. If singing isn't your thing, just stand with us. The words are on screen and there's no performance expected.

Hear the Word

The message is the heart of the service — a verse-by-verse walk through Scripture that's honest, practical, and centered on the Gospel. Bring a Bible or follow along on your phone.

Church Center App

Stay connected all week

We use the Church Center app for streamlined communication, secure child check-in, and staying in the loop with everything happening at Radiant. Download it before your first visit to make Sunday morning smooth.
