What Is a Gospel Community?

Church happens all week long

A Gospel Community (GC) is a small group of people — usually 8 to 16 — who gather regularly in someone's home to study Scripture, pray together, share meals, and encourage one another to live out the Gospel in everyday life.

At Radiant, we believe that Sunday is the gathering place, but GCs are where deep discipleship actually happens. Life in a GC means you know people and are known — you bear one another's burdens, celebrate together, and hold each other accountable to following Jesus.

GCs meet in various locations around Fairbanks on different days and times. Contact us to find a group that fits your schedule and neighborhood.

What to Expect

Life in a Gospel Community

Scripture & Discussion

Each week the group studies God's Word together — often reflecting on the Sunday sermon or working through a book of the Bible. Discussion, questions, and honest engagement are all welcome.

Prayer Together

GCs pray for one another — for health, jobs, families, and spiritual growth. Sharing prayer needs builds trust and reminds us that we depend on God in all of life.

Shared Life

Beyond the formal meeting, GC members do life together — sharing meals, helping each other move, checking in during hard weeks, and serving the neighborhood as a missional unit.
