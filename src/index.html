Welcome

A community gathered around the Gospel

We are a church in Fairbanks, Alaska, devoted to seeing the glory of God displayed through the good news of Jesus Christ. Whether you're exploring faith for the first time or looking for a place to grow deeper, we'd love to welcome you in.

Every Sunday we gather to worship, hear from God's Word, and encourage one another. We believe the Gospel isn't just how we get saved — it's how we live every day.
