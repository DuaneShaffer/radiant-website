Statewide

Partner in Alaska

In 2007, Caleb Richardson planted Radiant Church in Fairbanks. Since then, four other churches have joined the mission to see gospel-centered churches planted across Alaska. The plan was never to see Radiant become a brand — rather, we wanted to invite others into our family who desired the same vision, language, culture, and values. Together our vision is to be a radiant reflection of the Gospel to our cities and throughout the North: Gospel Communities who multiply disciples and churches. We want to see Gospel saturation across the north.
