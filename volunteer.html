Join a Team

Serving is discipleship in action. Use your gifts to build up the church and love your neighbors.

Why Serve?

Every member a minister

We believe every person in the church has been gifted by the Holy Spirit to build up the body of Christ. Serving on a team isn't just volunteering — it's a core part of what it means to be a disciple who is being equipped and deployed for the work of ministry.

Whether you love working with kids, setting up chairs, playing an instrument, or welcoming strangers — there is a place for you to serve at Radiant. Getting involved in a team is one of the best ways to grow deeper in community and put your faith into practice.

Ministry Areas

Where you can serve

Hospitality & Welcome

Greet guests at the door, help newcomers feel at home, and create a warm atmosphere before and after the service.

Worship Team

Musicians, vocalists, and sound technicians who lead the congregation in corporate worship each Sunday.

Family Ministry

Serve children from infancy through middle school. Background check and brief training required for all volunteers.

Setup & Teardown

Arrive early or stay after to help set up chairs, tables, and equipment — faithful, behind-the-scenes service that makes everything run smoothly.

Audio / Visual / Media

Run sound, manage slides, operate cameras, or help with livestream and social media during Sunday services.

Community Outreach

Serve Fairbanks through organized outreach initiatives — food distribution, neighborhood service projects, and more.

Ready to serve?

Email us with your name and area of interest. We'll connect you with the right team leader and walk you through next steps.
